About Dataset

“Access to safe drinking-water is essential to health, a basic human right and a component of effective policy for health protection. This is important as a health and development issue at a national, regional and local level.”

Variables

  • pH value: PH is an important parameter in evaluating the acid–base balance of water

  • Hardness: Hardness is mainly caused by calcium and magnesium salts.

  • Solids (Total dissolved solids - TDS): A wide range of inorganic and some organic minerals or salts such as potassium, calcium, sodium, bicarbonates, chlorides, magnesium, sulfates etc can be produced giving un-wanted taste and diluted color.

  • Chloramines: Chlorine and chloramine are the major disinfectants used in public water systems

  • Sulfate: Sulfates are naturally occurring substances that are found in minerals, soil, and rocks

  • Conductivity: Pure water is not a good conductor of electric current but rather is a good insulator.

  • Organic_carbon: Total Organic Carbon (TOC) in source waters comes from decaying natural organic matter (NOM) as well as synthetic sources.

  • Trihalomethanes: THMs are chemicals which may be found in water treated with chlorine.

  • Turbidity: The turbidity of water depends on the quantity of solid matter present in the suspended state.

  • Potability: Indicates if water is safe for human consumption
